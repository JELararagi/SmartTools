About this tool

UVs are coordinates that open a 3D surface flat, like a sheet of paper, to decide where an image is placed. SmartUV Suite gathers the buttons and settings for creating, adjusting, and arranging these UVs in the "Smart UV" tab of the Sidebar. The panel contains, in order, "Always-on Toggles", "Basic Settings", "UV Unwrap", "UV Align", "Packing", "Bake", "Operation", "Manage", and "Keymap Info". Every section header can be opened or closed by clicking its triangle or its title. Click the "?" to the right of a header to see an explanation of that section. Most operations affect only the UVs selected in the UV Editor.

1. What This Add-on Does

An add-on that puts everything into a single "Smart UV" tab in the Sidebar: "Auto Seam", which decides seam positions automatically, plus unwrapping, symbol-button alignment, auto packing with 18 presets, and baking.

  • Unwrap the UVs of the selected part with a single button ("Run Unwrap") after choosing a method
  • Mark and clear seams (the lines that cut UVs open) by hand, add them automatically, or add them from crease values
  • Align UV vertices and islands (connected clumps of UVs) to the edges or center, distribute them evenly, flip them, and rotate them
  • Straighten UVs, make them circular, relax them (even out stretching), and stitch them together
  • Choose a preset and pack UVs into an image without gaps (auto packing)
  • Create a UV map and image for baking, and bake material colors into the image
  • Create a checker (black-and-white grid) image to check UV stretching and size differences by eye
  • Measure and equalize texel density (how fine the UVs are)
  • Add, rename, and reorder the multiple UV maps (sets of UV coordinates) on one object
  • Count, select, and fix UV problems such as overlaps, flipped faces, and zero-area faces
  • Toggle features that assist with actions such as dragging right after click selection in the UV Editor
When to Use It
  • When creating UVs for a model for the first time: mark seams, then open the mesh with "Run Unwrap"
  • When tidying the orientation and arrangement of unwrapped UVs: use "Direction Align" or "Line / Flow" in "UV Align"
  • When combining UVs into one image for a texture: use "Run Auto Pack" in "Packing"
  • When checking whether UVs are stretched: create a checker with "Preview / Checker"
  • When combining material colors into one image: click the buttons in "Bake" > "Bake Prep" in order
  • When matching the numbers and names of UV maps used across multiple objects: use "Map Channels"

2. Key Features

This chapter walks through the features unique to this add-on, using numbered callouts on each figure. The numbers in a figure match the numbered list below it. Click a figure to enlarge it. Each individual setting is described under its own heading in Chapter 5.

2-1. Auto SeamTop Selling Point

To open UVs, you have to decide where to cut (the seams), which is where beginners get stuck the most. "Auto Seam" scores creases in the shape, open borders, material changes, sharp marks, and the like to pick edges and mark seams. It also adds the minimum cuts needed to open creaseless tubes and spheres and donut shapes with a hole through them flat, so you can open them just by clicking "Run Unwrap" next. Simply choose from 8 purposes such as "Hard Surface", "Organic", and "Tube/Pipe", and the detailed values switch all at once.

How to Use
  1. Select the mesh and select everything in Edit Mode.
  2. Open "UV Unwrap" > "Seam / Pin" > "Auto Seam Advanced" and choose the purpose that suits the shape in ④ "Auto Seam Preset" ("Hard Surface" for things with many hard creases, "Organic" for things whose large curved surfaces you don't want cut, and "Tube/Pipe" for tubes).
  3. Click ⑩ "Auto Seam" and check the seams made as red lines.
  4. Click "Run Unwrap" in "Smart Unwrap".
What's New
  • ⑪ "Seam by Angle", which looks only at crease angles, makes no lines on tubes, spheres, or donut shapes. ⑩ "Auto Seam" adds the cuts needed to open them as well.
  • Raise ⑤ "Adoption Score" when there are too many lines, and lower it when there are too few.
  • If you then click "Run Auto Pack" in "Packing", you can go from unwrapping to arranging without thinking about seams.

Settings in detail: 5-10. Seam/Pin, 5-8. Smart Unwrap

2-2. Auto Packing (18 Presets)Shown in Figures

Packing, which arranges islands in an image without gaps, is done in one go by choosing from 18 presets that suit different purposes. Choosing a preset switches the values for rotation, margin, UDIM, groups, and more all at once, so you don't have to learn the detailed settings: you can choose by name, such as "Dense Pack", "Pack by Material", "Stack Identical Islands + Pack", "UDIM Tile Pack", and "Lightmap Pack". The two sets below are the before and after of arranging the UVs of a cube with "Pack" and with "Stack Identical Islands + Pack".

How to Use
  1. In the UV Editor, select completely the faces of the islands you want to arrange.
  2. In "Packing" > "Auto Packing", choose the arrangement in ① "Preset".
  3. Click ② "Run Auto Pack".
Good to Know
  • When running, the values currently shown in ③ to ⑤ are used. You can choose a preset, change a few values, and then run.
  • Choose the preset named for your goal: "Stack Same Islands + Pack" to save image space by stacking islands of the same shape, or "UDIM Tile Pack" to split across several images.
  • Only islands whose faces are completely selected are arranged. Islands you don't want arranged stay put if you leave them unselected.

Settings in detail: 5-19. Packing

2-3. Align Direction (Symbol Buttons)Shown in Figures

The buttons for aligning the selected UVs or islands to an edge or the center, spacing them evenly, and flipping or rotating them are gathered in one place. Instead of text, the buttons show symbols such as arrows that indicate the direction of movement, and the direction of the arrow you press is the direction of the result. Symbols alone are hard to read, so the figure below shows what each button does.

How to Use
  1. In the UV Editor, select the UVs you want to align.
  2. In ① "Target" under "UV Align" > "Direction Align", choose "Vertex" or "Island".
  3. Click the button for the direction you want to move, from ② to ⑧.
Good to Know
  • When aligning with "Island", the position perpendicular to the alignment direction stays the same, so islands of similar height may overlap.
  • The buttons marked "G" in ④ and ⑥ appear only when ① "Target" is "Vertex".

5. Features

Each heading is explained in order from the top of the panel. Click the "?" to the right of a heading to show the same explanation in Blender. The blue "Example" boxes show real operations on simple models such as Suzanne, with the 3D Viewport before and after.

5-1. Always-on Toggles

The area at the very top of the panel that groups on / off switches for features that keep working in the background. It contains no one-shot operations. It is closed at first. The button at the right of each row shows the current state as "On" or "Off", and clicking it toggles the state.

Location"Smart UV" tab > "Always-on Toggles" at the very top (clock icon)

ItemWhat it does
"N-panel State Save"When enabled, every change to the panel settings is saved to a file in the Blender configuration folder and restored after Blender is reopened. While disabled, nothing is saved. It is enabled at first.
"Live Unwrap"Keeps Blender's Live Unwrap enabled (a feature that automatically updates pinned UVs while you edit seams).
"UV Sync Selection"Makes the mesh selection in the 3D Viewport and the selection in the UV Editor the same. Disable it when you want to select UVs separately.
"Smart Auto UV Sync"Remembers the preferred state of SmartUV Suite's automatic UV sync. Enable it only when needed.
"Smart Unwrap Realtime Update"Lets the previous unwrap be redone automatically when you change the unwrap settings. Disable it when you want lighter editing.
"Transform Protection"Installs a mechanism that removes leftover SmartUV Suite markers from old files while keeping the object's location, rotation, and scale. Enable it only when needed for old files.
"Blender 5.1 Compatibility Repair"Installs a mechanism that fixes old values left in startup settings and similar places when they cause warnings in Blender 5.1. Enable it only when warnings appear.
"Instant Select Drag Move", "Axis Constraint Memory", "UV Gizmo Grab Assist"These are the same switches as the features of the same names in the "Operation" section. You can toggle them from here as well.
Note
Merely opening a section does not enable any of its features.

6. Common Tasks

Unwrap and Pack the UVs of a Cube

Mark seams, unwrap, and arrange the result neatly in the image

  1. Select the cube and press the Tab key to enter Edit Mode.
  2. Switch to edge select (the 2 key) and select the edges you want to cut open.
  3. Click "Mark Seam" in "UV Unwrap" > "Seam / Pin".
  4. Press the A key to select everything, and select all the UVs in the UV Editor as well.
  5. In "Smart Unwrap", choose "Angle Based Unwrap" for "Method" and click "Run Unwrap".
  6. In "Packing" > "Auto Packing", choose "Pack" for "Preset" and click "Run Auto Pack".

Result: The UVs are cut open at the seams and arranged within the image bounds with gaps between them.

Unwrap and Pack Without Thinking About Seams

Leave the cuts to "Auto Seam" and go straight from unwrapping to packing

  1. Select the object, press the Tab key to enter Edit Mode, and press the A key to select everything.
  2. Click "Auto Seam" in "UV Unwrap" > "Seam / Pin" > "Auto Seam Advanced".
  3. Click "Run Unwrap" in "Smart Unwrap".
  4. Click "Run Auto Pack" in "Packing" > "Auto Packing".

Result: The mesh is cut open with seams that follow the creases of the shape, and the islands are arranged in the frame without overlapping.

Check Stretching with a Checker

See UV stretching and size differences by eye

  1. Select the object you want to check.
  2. In "Basic Settings" > "Preview / Checker", set "Image Resolution Width", "Image Resolution Height", "Checker Tiles U", and "Checker Tiles V".
  3. Click "Create Checker".
  4. Click "Apply to Materials". If you set the 3D Viewport display to "Material Preview", the grid appears on the object (this button does not change the 3D Viewport display itself).
  5. When you have finished checking, click "Restore Materials", and click "Delete Checker" if you no longer need it.

Result: Where the grid squares are squashed or differ in size, you can see the stretching and the density differences.

Align Islands to the Left Edge

Align the left edges of the selected islands

  1. Select the islands you want to align in the UV Editor.
  2. Click "Island" under "Target" in "UV Align" > "Direction Align".
  3. Click the "←" button.

Result: The selected islands line up at the left edge while keeping their shape.

Match the UV Map Numbers and Names of Multiple Objects

Match the UV maps used for unwrapping across multiple objects

  1. Select all the mesh objects you want to match.
  2. In the list in "Basic Settings" > "Map Channels", click the row of the object to use as the reference.
  3. Choose the UV map to use in "Unwrap Map Channel".
  4. Turn on the checkbox at the left of the rows of the objects you want to match.
  5. Click "Unify Checked".

Result: The numbers and names of the unwrap UV maps of the checked objects become the same as those of the reference object.

Bake Material Colors into One Image

Combine the colors of multiple objects into one image and switch to that material

  1. Select all the objects you want to combine.
  2. Set "Texture Size" and "Bake Margin" in "Bake" > "Bake Prep".
  3. Click "Prepare Bake UV". A UV map named "UV_Bake" is created and the islands are arranged in the frame.
  4. Click "Atlas Bake". The colors are baked into the image "SmartUV_Bake".
  5. Click "Create Material".

Result: The objects switch to the material "SmartUV_Baked", which uses the baked image, and the look stays the same as the original colors. The image is saved inside the .blend file. If you change the original material colors, click "Atlas Bake" again and the image changes to the new colors.

7. Troubleshooting

I can't find the "Smart UV" tab.
Press the N key in the UV Editor or the 3D Viewport to show the Sidebar, and look for the vertical tab. Which editors show the panel is set in the layout settings of Smart Tools Manager, so check there as well.
I click "Run Unwrap" but the UVs don't change.
When "UV Sync Selection" is on, the target is the faces selected in Edit Mode; when it is off, the target is the UVs selected in the UV Editor. When it is off and no UVs are selected, all faces shown in the UV Editor are the target. If nothing is shown in the UV Editor, select faces in Edit Mode.
When I changed the method, the settings fields below changed.
This is normal. Only the settings used by the chosen "Method" are shown. For example, "Smart Unwrap Settings" appears for "Detailed Unwrap", and "Shape Method Settings" appears for "Square" and "Circle".
Buttons such as "G—" in Align Direction don't appear.
The per-group buttons appear only when "Target" is "Vertex". They do not appear when it is "Island".
"Move Up" and "Rename Channel" can't be clicked (they are grayed out).
"Rename Channel" can't be clicked when there is no UV map. "Move Up" and "Move Down" can't be clicked when there is only one UV map or when it is already at the very top or bottom. If there is no UV map, create one first with "Add".
Are the panel's open/closed states and settings kept the next time I open Blender?
If "N-panel State Save" in "Always-on Toggles" is "On", they are saved with every change and restored after Blender is reopened. If it is "Off", nothing is saved.
Some UVs don't move when I click "Run Auto Pack".
Auto packing arranges only the islands whose faces are completely selected in the UV Editor. Check that all the UVs you want to move are selected.
When I changed the preset, the values of the settings below changed too.
This is normal. When you choose a preset, the settings below switch to that preset's values. When running, the values shown below are used, so correct anything you want to change before running.
I clicked "Normalize Density", but the differences in pattern size between faces are not evened out.
"Normalize Density" scales all the selected UVs up or down by the same factor. To even out the size ratio between islands, use "Average Scale" in "UV Unwrap" > "Native Assist / Flatten".
Does "Auto Seam" add lines even to spheres and tubes that have no corners?
Yes. Even for shapes with no crease angle to go on, it adds the minimum cuts needed to open closed shapes, shapes with holes through them, and tubes flat.
When I clicked "Prepare Bake UV", the arrangement of the UVs changed.
This is because it created a new UV map, "UV_Bake", and rearranged all the islands in the frame there. The original UV map is left as is. You can switch the working UV map back in "Map Channels".
I click "Validate" but the UVs don't change.
"Validate" only counts the problems and shows the counts at the bottom of the screen. To select the problem faces, click "Select Issues"; to fix them, click "Fix Issues".
When I click "Select by Area" or "Texel Density", a notice asks me to select a reference.
Select one island to use as the reference first, then click.
I changed the original colors after "Create Material". Can I bake again?
Click "Atlas Bake" again. It rebakes from the original material colors and renews the image. "SmartUV_Baked" stays applied to the faces. Faces whose original material slot has been deleted can't be rebaked, so a notice appears and it stops.
When I click "Create Material", it says 'Click "Atlas Bake" first.'
This is because no colors have been baked into the image yet. "Create Bake Image" only creates an empty image, so click "Atlas Bake" and then click "Create Material".

For problems common to every add-on, such as an add-on not appearing or the interface language not changing, see "Troubleshooting" in the SmartTools Overall Specification.

8. Glossary

UV
Coordinates that open the surface of a 3D shape flat and decide which part of an image is applied where.
UV Map / Map Channel
A set of UV coordinates that an object has. One object can have several.
Unwrap
Cutting open the surface of a 3D shape to create UVs.
Seam
An edge marked as a line to cut open when unwrapping.
Pin
A UV point marked so that it does not move when unwrapping.
Island
A connected clump within the UVs.
Packing (Pack)
Arranging UV islands in an image without gaps.
Stitch
Sewing together UV edges or islands that have been cut apart.
Texel Density
How much image detail the UVs assign relative to the 3D size.
UDIM
A system that divides the UV range into tiles of multiple images.
Checker
An image of a black-and-white or similar grid. Applying it makes UV stretching and size differences visible.
Bake
Burning colors, shading, and the like into an image.
Keymap
A list of assignments between key or mouse actions and functions. You can view it in Preferences.
Gizmo
Handles shown on screen for moving, rotating, and scaling.

Limitations and Notes

  • UV operations such as unwrapping, aligning, and packing affect only the selected UVs ("Run Unwrap" with "UV Sync Selection" off and no UVs selected affects all faces shown in the UV Editor).
  • "Run Auto Pack" arranges only islands whose faces are completely selected in the UV Editor, and does not change UVs that are not selected.
  • "2D Cursor to Hover Vertex" cannot be used unless you register it yourself in the Preferences keymap.
  • The per-group buttons in Direction Align (such as "G—") appear only when "Target" is "Vertex".
  • "Grid Divisions" does not appear when "Grid Basis" is "Dynamic" or "Pixel".
  • The settings in "UV Editor Ownership" affect only the UV Editor.
  • "Set Density" and "Normalize Density" scale all the selected UVs up or down by the same factor, so the size ratio between islands does not change.
  • "Mirror UV", "Symmetry Snap", and "Mirrored 3D" work only on faces of meshes that are symmetrical about the object's X axis.
  • "Atlas Bake" bakes only material colors, using Cycles. To bake shading or normals, use the bake in Blender's "Render" properties.
  • "Fix Problems" does not fix zero-area faces. It also does not move UVs outside the frame (because they may be used in UDIM).
  • "Load Seams", "Load Layout", and "Load Selection" can be used only on a mesh with the same face structure as when it was saved.
  • "Smart Stack" treats only shapes that overlap after rotation as the same shape. Shapes flipped left-right are treated as different shapes.
  • When you use "Atlas Bake" on multiple objects at once, match the names of the working UV maps. Clicking "Prepare Bake UV" matches them as "UV_Bake".
